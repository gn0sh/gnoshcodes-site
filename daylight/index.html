What's there

Look it up. Read the source.

Here is what you can do on Daylight today, and what is not there yet.

Who represents you

  • Enter your street address to see your city ward and your county district.
  • Or pick from the list of 12 wards and 21 districts.

How they voted

  • Recorded votes of the Clarksville City Council and the Montgomery County Commission, by meeting and by member.
  • Each vote links to the page of the minutes it came from.

Meetings and agendas

  • The agenda before a meeting. The minutes and votes after it, once they are posted.
  • Regional Planning Commission meetings, with the rezoning cases on each agenda.

Public money

  • Montgomery County's budget books, fund by fund.
  • The bids and awards the county purchasing office lists.

Search

  • Search the records by street, name, topic or item number.
  • The front page shows how recent each body's records are.

Not there yet

  • School board records and the city budget are not collected yet.
  • Regional Planning Commission votes are not shown yet.
  • Daylight marks each of these “Coming” and links the official source.

How it works

Every fact links to its source.

  1. Collected from the source

    Daylight reads the documents each public body posts online, such as agendas, minutes and budget books, and keeps each one unchanged.

  2. Checked before it's shown

    A computer program reads each document, and a program can misread a page. So each fact is checked automatically. A roll call that fails a check is marked “Not confirmed” and no vote from it is shown.

  3. Linked to the page

    Every fact links to its source document, opened at the page where the fact appears, and shows the date the document was fetched.

  4. Corrected in the open

    Every fact has a Report an error link. Each report is checked against the source document, and each fix is recorded on the site's Changes page.

Who runs it

Independent. Unofficial.

Daylight is an independent, unofficial site run by gnoshcodes LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the City of Clarksville, Montgomery County, the Clarksville-Montgomery County School System (CMCSS), or the Clarksville-Montgomery County Regional Planning Commission. The official record held by each body controls.

The same rules for everyone

gnoshcodes LLC is run by Brian Zacharias, who is on the Clarksville City Council (Ward 1). This member's votes are shown by the same rules as every other official's.

Every official is shown with the same layout, the same fields and the same order. Only facts are shown. Nothing is ordered by a count, and nothing is turned into a judgment.

Daylight is free to read. There are no advertisements, no campaign links and no visitor tracking.
