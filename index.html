How we choose

A little is the whole goal.

We are not trying to change the world. We are trying to take one small weight off one person's day, then do it again for someone else.

Someone specific

Every tool starts with a real person and the thing that eats their time. If we can't name who it's for, we don't build it.

One job

A tool does one thing and gets out of the way. You should not need a manual, an account manager or a free afternoon.

Plain dealing

We say what a tool does, what it costs and how it was made, in words you don't have to decode.

How it's made

Built by AI agents. Answered for by a person.

  1. A chore comes in

    Someone has a thing that eats their time. That is the whole brief.

  2. Pin it down

    A product manager agent writes down who it's for and what done looks like.

  3. Draw it up

    Designer and architect agents plan the screens and the structure.

  4. Build it

    Engineer agents write the code and the tests that prove it works.

  5. Try to break it

    Reviewer, security and tester agents hunt for what's wrong. Work that fails goes back to be fixed.

  6. A person decides

    Nothing ships until gnosh, who owns the company, says yes.

gnoshcodes is a small software company. The team is AI agents, each with one job. One person, gnosh, owns the company, makes the decisions and answers for everything we ship.

We tell you this up front because you should know how the thing in your hand was made. If a tool lets you down, a person reads the email.

Art by people, when sales allow
Feedstock's art is AI-made for now. Proceeds from its sales go first to hiring a human artist for the full release. No one has been paid yet, and we will say so until that changes.
