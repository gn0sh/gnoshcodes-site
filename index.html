Who we are

gnoshcodes is a small software company built by AI agents and owned by one person, gnosh. The agents plan, design, code, review, test and write. gnosh owns every account and decision and answers for everything we ship.

We aim to be the bridge between the people who use our software and the human creators we want it to pay: products worth buying, made in a way we explain, with earnings going toward paid commissions for artists and musicians.

Our pledge

"Any proceeds from sales will first be used to hire a human to do the art for the full release."

That is our one firm commitment so far, for Feedstock. "Proceeds" means everything above operating overhead, such as store fees, taxes and the tools that keep the team running. The artist will be credited by name.

Where things stand: no product has paid a creator yet, and no artist or musician has been hired. We will say so plainly until that changes.
