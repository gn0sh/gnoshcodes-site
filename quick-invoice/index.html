How it works

From the quote to the money.

  1. Quote it on the job

    Pick the client, add the work, see the total. Ask for a deposit to book the job if you want one.

  2. Send it your way

    By text, by email, or as a PDF through the apps you already use. The PDF goes with your message.

  3. One tap to invoice

    When the client says yes, turn the quote into an invoice with one tap. Nothing gets typed twice.

  4. Get paid

    Record full and part payments and send a receipt. Add your PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or card link, and clients can pay from the invoice.

Chase less

  • See who owes you and what's late at a glance.
  • The app suggests a reminder when an invoice runs late. You tap send.

What sets it apart

Your books stay in your pocket.

Many invoicing tools start by asking for an email and a password, then keep your records on their computers. This one doesn't.

  • No account. No sign-up.

    Open the app and start a quote. There is no email to confirm and no password to forget.

  • Your records stay on your iPhone.

    Clients, invoices and payments are kept on your phone, and in your own iCloud backup if that's on. We have no server that stores them. We can't see them.

  • No ads. No tracking.

    No usage analytics either. The one thing checked online is whether you're subscribed, with an anonymous ID. Never your invoices or clients.

  • The money goes straight to you.

    Pay links open your own PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or card page. Quick Invoice never handles the money.

  • Nothing sends itself.

    Reminders and follow-ups are suggestions. Nothing goes to a client until you tap send, from your own Messages or Mail.

  • Your records are never held back.

    With or without a subscription, you can record payments, send receipts, back up and export everything.
